How Numco Works
1. Normal Numco

Use symbols from only one column for the entire message. You cannot switch columns mid-message.
"-8" (Col 1), "—⁸" (Col 2), "°×" (Col 3), "′×" (Col 4).

2. Alternating Numco

Mix symbols from different columns, where every symbol comes from a different column than the one right before it.
Example: "-×" (- is Col 1, × is Col 3 or 4).
